Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

PS5 Controller Ko Console Ke Saath Pehli Baar Pair Kaise Karein?

Apne controller ko original USB Type-C cable ke zariye PS5 console se connect karein, fir controller par central PS Button ko press karein. Player indicator light light-up hote hi connection register ho jayega.

Kya PS5 Controller PC Ya Laptop Ke Saath Kaam Karta Hai?

Haan! Aap controller ke PS Button aur Create (Share) Button ko ek sath press karke Bluetooth pairing mode active kar sakte hain aur ise direct PC, Mac, ya Mobile device ke sath connect kar sakte hain.

Controller Ke Stick Drift Ya Disconnection Issue Ko Kaise Fix Karein?

Controller ke back panel par small screw hole ke paas ek pinhole reset button hota hai. Kisi paperclip ya tool se us reset button ko 5 seconds tak press karke rakhein, uske baad cable se reconnect karein.
