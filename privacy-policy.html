Privacy Policy for tvremoteshop.in

At TV Remote Shop, accessible from https://tvremoteshop.in, safeguarding the privacy of our digital visitors is an absolute priority. This comprehensive Privacy Policy defines the parameters of informational logging, user data protection, and external advertising disclosures implemented on our platform.

When placing an order for products including Sony, Samsung, LG, Mi, or TCL replacement remotes, we process basic customer data essential to execute courier logistics. This securely encompasses your Name, Delivery/Shipping Address, Contact Phone Number, and Email Address. Financial transactions are tokenized via audited third-party encrypted merchant gateways.

tvremoteshop.in follows standard server architecture utilizing automated log files. These files catalog routine ISP configurations, entry/exit timestamp analytics, and browser structures. 3. Google AdSense & Cookie Regulations

Google is integrated as a primary third-party vendor on our infrastructure. Google utilizes tracking networks, including the DART cookie matrix, to serve target advertisements to our audience based upon behavioral searches on this platform and auxiliary portals across the web. Users can choose to opt-out or alter custom parameters by reviewing the corporate Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policies.

4. Data Rights Compliance

We strictly align with international digital protection paradigms. Our users preserve full absolute rights to request information extraction access, modify stored shipping points, or request absolute deletion of profile datasets from our server databases.
